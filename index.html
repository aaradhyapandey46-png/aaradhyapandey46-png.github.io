a little about it

how i edit

I spend most of my time inside long, unstructured footage, the kind where the good stuff is buried between three minutes of nothing. My job is finding it, then pacing it so it feels like a story, not a dump of clips. I talk to every creator before I start cutting, so the video still sounds like you when it's done, just tighter and better paced.

before you ask

a few things

$35 per long-form video. That covers editing your raw footage, pacing, transitions, basic color, and sound design. If your video has extra moving parts, just tell me your budget and we'll figure it out.
Usually 4 to 6 days once I have your footage and a rough sense of what you want.
Your footage (Drive or Dropbox is fine) and a sense of the vibe, bullet points work, you don't need a full script.
